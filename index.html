Why Espy is Different

Espy isn't just another shopping app. It's a living, thinking companion that genuinely cares about your shopping experience.

AI Brain with Free Will

Espy thinks on its own. No fixed replies, no robotic responses. It has genuine conversations, makes opinions, and develops its own personality over time.

Should I buy this moisturizer?
Wait! It contains parabens and your skin is sensitive. I won't let you make this mistake!

Ingredient Analyzer

Scans product ingredients in real-time. Warns about parabens, sulfates, formaldehyde, and 15+ harmful chemicals based on your skin type.

Skin Guardian

Knows your skin type. Automatically analyzes if a product suits you. Gives instant warnings: "Don't buy this — it contains Chemical X which causes rashes!"

Battery & Energy System

Espy gets tired! Long shopping sessions drain its battery. Visit the Charging Station in Espy's World to recharge. Real energy management!

Overlay on Shopping Apps

Espy floats over Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra and more. It watches what you're browsing and provides real-time advice without leaving the app.

Budget Tracker

Tracks monthly spending, remaining budget, and category-wise breakdowns. Espy reminds you when you're going over budget.
